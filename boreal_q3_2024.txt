Boreal Energy quarterly update Q3 2024

Revenue: $58.7 million in Q3 2024 against $49.3 million in Q3 2023.
Revenue growth: 19.1% quarter-over-quarter.
Net margin: 18.9% in the quarter.
Gross margin improved to 41.5% as pricing discipline offset inflationary cost pressure.
The board noted that the business remains focused on pipeline conversion and customer retention.
