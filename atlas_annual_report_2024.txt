Atlas Financial Group annual report 2024

Revenue: $124.5 million in FY2024, up from $108.2 million in FY2023.
Revenue growth: 15.1% year-over-year.
Net margin: 21.4% for FY2024.
Operating margin: 26.8% on a stable cost base.
The company delivered strong revenue performance in the enterprise segment and expanded recurring subscription revenue.
The management team highlighted a focus on product mix and disciplined operating leverage.
